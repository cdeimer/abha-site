After the City's snow removal contractors destroyed the Western Ave and North Beacon St bike lane vertical separation (flexposts) in January 2026, then-interim Chief of Streets Nick Gove refused to meet with or even respond to Allston-Brighton residents. This was disrespectful and dismissive of the dangers that we experience on our roads on a daily basis. We are heartened by the more earnest communication under new Interim Chief of Streets, Chris Osgood, and his team over the last few months, which has included meeting with residents and a commitment to restore the bike lane separation. The Mayor reaffirmed this commitment at a meeting with Allston-Brighton street safety advocates in late September.

This past weekend the Streets Cabinet began reinstalling flexposts on Western Ave (work that is expected to be completed this week). This will immediately improve safety for everyone who walks, bikes, takes the bus, and drives on Western Ave. We'd like to particularly thank Tali Robbins, interim Chief of Staff of the Streets Cabinet, who did much of the work to coordinate the reinstallation.

Replacing damaged safe streets infrastructure that was fought for and won by Allston-Brighton residents is, however, the bare minimum. While we are thankful for the restored protection on Western Ave, the flexposts in the North Beacon Street bike lane still have not been replaced. The Administration has wasted two years by pausing projects and paralyzing street safety efforts, and we expect to see sustained action by the Streets Cabinet to both replace damaged infrastructure AND continue to reimagine our streets to make them safer for people using all modes of transportation. We will continue to demand that the administration make specific, meaningful commitments to street safety and that they follow through on them in a timely and transparent manner.
